At the top right of your <span style="color:#6893FF">**menubar**</span>, you can see the window minimize button, maximize window button and the close button.

Note: the close button will only close open subwindows in this specific window, in case a single project has multiple windows open. To close everything, press Ctrl while clicking on this button.

To the left of theses three buttons you will see optional information such as your project name, messages or other little informations.

Below the three main window buttons, you can also see the little close button of the active window attached to the <span style="color:#FF68C8">**dockspace**</span>.

## Navigate the Editor

On the menubar, the "Tools" menu can open main tools such as the content browser or logs utility windows, but also some installed module utilities.
The "Project" menu gives access to the project settings, projects modules and plugins. 

You see every open window of your project in the <span style="color:#FF68C8">**dockspace**</span>. 

You can also find every window’s attached titlebar on the menubar just above the dockspace.

## Dock and undock windows

A cool feature of Vortex is that you can easily dock, undock, drag and redock all the windows of your editor! And then have multiple windows open of the same project, with different views, a side content browser or logs terminal to dynamize your experience.

<image
  src="https://static.infinite.si/vxdocs/1.0/get_started/media/editor/undock.gif"
  type="bordered"
/>

You simply need to drag the titlebar of a window and drop it wherever you want, in the void to create a new main window, or in another existing window to organize your workspace.
