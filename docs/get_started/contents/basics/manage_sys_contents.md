The Vortex Launcher allows you to manage your system contents such as modules/plugins/items/templates. Managing these in the system from the launcher lets you easily import them into all of your projects, without an internet connection.

Warning: Reminder that contents downloaded from outside are pieces of programs/scripts from others, you need to trust every content that you install.

A content installed in your system is only available to your projects, it's not executed directly, this allows you to use only what you need in your projects, without importing all of your contents. You can then have multiple projects/tools of different natures.

### Install from the Garage

The Infinite Garage is a sharing platform for makers, aimed at simplifying the process of creating and developing projects. It offers a variety of tools, modules, plugins and resources to help creators enhance productivity.

You can search for modules, plugins, contents or templates and directly import them into your system to be ready for import into all of your projects.

In order to import any type of content, click on the download button of any content, and copy the code, then in your Vortex Launcher system content manager (available in the Vortex menu at the top), click on enter code with the garage logo and paste the code. If the code is valid you will see the content appearing, click on Download and Install to install it directly on your system. After that, this content will be accessible in your projects/tools if compatible!

### Import manually

You can still import any content manually, by going to the System content manager window (available in the Vortex menu at the top of the Vortex Launcher), and clicking on import. You will see every content on your own filesystem that you can import for the selected type of content.

You can even import any content manually by simply adding the content folder in the right folder (see all your system paths in the settings window, in the section System paths). Please be careful to import the right folder at the right place.

### Delete a content

You can delete any content by going to the system content manager, and hitting the red trash button. (This will delete only the content registered in the system, not the ones in your projects.)
