The project settings window allows you to configure your project and customize custom settings. You can also customize your experience by changing the Vortex Editor behavior, color theme, or project identity. Every setting is only for the current project; it doesn't impact other projects' settings.

### First look

In this window you simply have the sidebar with all menus and the content bar with settings to edit.

## Project information
You can change the project's information by clicking on the "Informations" menu in the sidebar.
Then, you will have access to all types of information to edit such as the name, description, authors, legal information or statements.

You can save your modifications by clicking on the save button, or refresh to reload your project information.

## Configure the project
You can configure your project, like the behavior of the content browser, the default project path or the startup script, in the Configurations and Startup sections in the sidebar.

## Customize the appearance
You can change the editor theme/appearance by going to Appearance in the sidebar. You can then switch the active theme between dark or light, or even use a custom theme.

## Network configurations
You can allow Vortex to connect to the internet (only to download Garage and community contents; Vortex will never upload information about your system/project to the web). This setting will set the flag for every installed editor, but you can deactivate it at any moment.
