The console logs utility gives you an overview of what's happening in the project. You can also execute Lua commands at the bottom, and directly interact with modules or other things.

### First look

This is the console logs utility. At the top you have the menubar, with the search feature and filter bar toggle at the left, and the view parameters at the right.

### Read logs

All logs come in the center of this window. You can configure the display type between simple (default), advanced or block. You can also copy logs to your clipboard with the "block view".

### Search specific logs

By clicking on the Search button at the top left in the menubar, you can toggle the search bar. Once you write something into it, the content section will show you only the logs that correspond to your search.

### Apply filters

By clicking on the filter button, you will toggle the filter bar at the left. Then, you can show only logs by their type (warning, errors, etc.), or filter by topic (modules, core or network features...).

### Execute commands

At the bottom bar, you have a command prompt, allowing you to execute custom commands targeting the Lua API of Vortex. You can then trigger custom events, and call plugin and module events.
