### Install versions of Vortex Editor

To open a project, you need a valid version of the Vortex Editor, to get the Vortex Editor, the Vortex Launcher allows you to download it directly.

In order to download a version of the Vortex Editor, go on the menubar and click on the Vortex menu, then the Vortex version utility.

<image
  src="https://static.infinite.si/vxdocs/1.0/get_started/media/launcher/version_manager.png"
  type="bordered"
/>

In the version manager you can find every versions of Vortex (depending of your distributions channels). Select the right version for your projects and hit Install.

Then a Version installer will open, follow the steps and restart the launcher, if everything is OK, you will be able to open your project.

---

### How projects works?

#### Independance of projects
Every project and tools are independant from each others ! Every modules and plugins that you install in a specific project are not installed for your entire Vortex installation.

This gives us two opportunity in the context of Vortex :
- **Multiply the project/tools natures :** Thanks to that approach, you can have many features integration without bloating your entire ecosystem, you can have a firmware project and a network debugger project without collide theses two context and have too much useless features for each context.
- **Sharing is easy**: You want to share your project with others creators ? Or event open it in another system ? So you dont need to reproduce your development environnement ! Because yes, your contents follows your project and not your system. Share, migration and collaboration is easy.

#### Settings
Like modules and plugins, settings are related to projects, and not system, so you can customize a different experience for each project you have, and better than that, you can share project with custom configurations !
